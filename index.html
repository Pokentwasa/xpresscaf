The big question

Why R14?

— Nicolene & Tomer Elhadad

Founders, Xpresso Café

Hello there!

We are Nicolene and Tomer Elhadad and we are the proud founders of Xpresso Café in South Africa. It has always been a dream for us to start a coffee offering that is affordable to everyone.

We wanted to spoil people with great coffee, at the best price. So why R14? Because why not? Everything in life does not have to be expensive — and yes, we do not make a lot of profit, but in life, giving has always been better than receiving.

Seeing our customers being able to come back each and every day for their favourite snack and coffee is a dream come true. Allowing everyone to experience what good roasted coffee should taste like is an honour in itself.

Life is short and we wanted to share this passion for coffee and yummy treats with as many people in SA as possible. Thank you for being here — we would love to see you in one of our stores soon.
