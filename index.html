Why we started

Good coffee shouldn't cost the earth.

We wanted the coffee shop experience to belong to everyone — not just whoever had the budget for it.

XPRESSO Café is a trendy, upmarket take-away café offering all South Africans the chance to taste the coffee shop experience at the best price possible. Flavourful coffee, delicious pastries, appetising sandwiches and decadent treats — any time of the day.
